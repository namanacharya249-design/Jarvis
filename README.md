# barehands 👐

**Move things on your screen with your bare hands.** `barehands` turns your webcam into a hand-tracked interface where notes, images, and 3D models float over your camera as glass cards. Pinch them, throw them, stretch them, force-pull them across the room, or blow an engine apart into its exploded view with a drag of two fingers. 

No headset. No controllers. No gloves. Just Bare hands.

## 🚀 Features

* **Zero Hardware Needed:** Runs entirely on a standard webcam and Google Chrome.
* **Spatial Interactions:** Pinch, throw, stretch, and force-pull virtual glass cards.
* **3D Exploded Views:** Deconstruct complex 3D models (like engines) using intuitive two-finger drag gestures.
* **Floating Interfaces:** Interact with notes, images, and 3D models seamlessly overlaid on your camera feed.
* **Jarvis AI Voice Commands:** Summon any image instantly using your voice and ask conversational questions about it.
