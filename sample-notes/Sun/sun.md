\# The Sun



The Sun is the star at the center of our Solar System.



\## What is the Sun?



The Sun is a huge ball of extremely hot plasma. It provides the light and heat that make life on Earth possible.



\## Temperature



\- Surface: about 5,500°C

\- Core: about 15 million°C



\## How does the Sun produce energy?



The Sun produces energy through \*\*nuclear fusion\*\*. In its core, hydrogen nuclei combine to form helium and release enormous amounts of energy.



\## Why is the Sun important?



The Sun provides:



\- Light

\- Heat

\- Energy for plants through photosynthesis

\- The main source of energy driving Earth's climate and weather



\## Interesting Fact



The Sun contains about \*\*99.8% of the total mass of the Solar System\*\*.
